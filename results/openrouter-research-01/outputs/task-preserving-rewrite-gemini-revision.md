### The Exact Unsolved Technical Bottleneck

No compelling construction survives. The core bottleneck is the **Subspace Leakage vs. Recomputation Dilemma**. 

For a task-preserving rewrite to offer utility, it must reuse the sender's computational work (the witness or trace). To provide a zero-capacity content channel, it must map all valid witnesses to a single, canonical output. 

If the receiver restricts its canonicalization search to the subspace generated by the sender's witness (to save compute), the final output remains functionally dependent on the sender's choice of subspace. This guarantees a non-zero total variation (TV > 0), leaving a steganographic channel open. Conversely, if the receiver expands the search to a global canonical form to close the channel, it must perform the full search itself, completely destroying the computational reuse advantage that justified the rewrite in the first place. 

Standard processing applied to the E-graph model demonstrates this perfectly: trace-order sensitivity and disjoint intermediate lemmas mean the restricted E-graph is merely a proxy for the sender's payload.
